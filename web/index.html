Reading the record

Filtered vegetation detections from NASA FIRMS are counted inside the selected rectangle. Province presets approximate their boundaries. The global map shows recent VIIRS observations independently of the historical date and season filters.

One comparable calendar

Before 2012, monthly MODIS counts are scaled by the selected area's median VIIRS-to-MODIS ratio during 2012–2025. Months with fewer than five usable overlap years use Pakistan's reference ratio. From 2012, each Suomi NPP grid cell (approximately 1 km) counts once per day. NOAA-20 appears in the point export.

Historical normal

Each month is compared with its 2001–2024 median. Partial months and zero baselines have no percentage comparison.

Policy comparisons

The checker uses complete seasons and the national model’s 15.8% held-out error as a reference threshold. Timing shifts and observation coverage affect the evidence. A before-and-after comparison cannot establish what caused a change.
